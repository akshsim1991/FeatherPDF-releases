# Feather PDF: privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Feather PDF (for Android, Windows and macOS) is made by Akshaya Simha. This policy says what the apps do with
information about you. In short: **nothing leaves your device, and nothing is collected.**

## What Feather PDF collects

Nothing. Feather PDF has no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting. It does not
collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or usage data.

## Your documents

Feather PDF opens only the documents you choose (with the system's file picker, "Open with" or "Share to"). They are
read and changed on your device only. To open a document, the app may keep a working copy in its own private storage
on the device; it is never sent anywhere. Saving writes the document only where you choose.

## What stays on your device

- The list of documents you opened lately, with the page you last read, and your settings (such as page colours). You
  can clear the list in the app at any time; uninstalling the app removes all of it.

## Internet

- **Windows, macOS and Android:** the app connects to the internet only to see whether a new version is out (it reads a small
  version file from GitHub, and from the jsDelivr mirror when GitHub cannot be reached), which you can turn off in
  Settings, and when you choose to download an update. No information about you or your documents is sent.

## Children

Feather PDF does not collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new date.
